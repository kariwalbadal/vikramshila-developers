# Phase 1 — Study

## Who they are
Vikramshila Developers Pvt. Ltd. is a small, family-scale real-estate developer working in Bhagalpur and Deoghar (Bihar/Jharkhand), with one outlier project in Kolkata. Their own copy states "over 1 lac sqft of landmark developments" and "founded over a decade(s) ago" — no exact year given. They build mid-rise (4–9 floor) residential apartment blocks, mostly 2BHK/3BHK, sold directly (no visible broker network, no RERA numbers published, no listed prices) — this is a founder/promoter-led regional developer, not a national brand. They also run one unrelated hospitality property (Hotel Chinmaye Inn) under the same promoter.

## What they build, for whom
Nine documented residential projects: Sunrise, Tejprabha Residency, Chandeshwar Apartment, Keshavam Apartment, Ganesh Enclave, Shivalaya, Annapurna Heights, Jagdish Enclave, plus three stub-only listings (Devpreet Vikramshila, Shyamri Tower, Astha Garden) with no source material to build pages from. The buyer is a Bihari/Jharkhandi family buying a first or second home — 2BHK ~1000–1250 sqft, 3BHK ~1350–1800 sqft — not luxury towers, not investment-grade skyscrapers. The "monument" framing has to come from craft and typography, not from pretending these are 40-storey landmarks.

## Strongest material
- **Photography/renders:** every project page has 2–5 CGI marketing renders (day/night/aerial) at ~1300–1600px. Quality is inconsistent — some (Shivalaya, Chandeshwar night view, Annapurna Heights night view) are genuinely striking dusk/twilight renders with warm interior light, dramatic sky, palm-lined streets. Others (Sunrise day render) are cruder, with obviously photo-bashed cars/figures. Floor-plan graphics and brochure PDFs exist for most projects.
- **Copy:** the company philosophy line — *"Creation, Not Construction"* — is the one piece of real brand voice worth building the whole site's tone around. It's understated, confident, and ties naturally to the Vikramshila-as-legend framing (a place of learning/making, not just building).
- **Trust markers actually present:** "250 Satisfied Families," "5 Active Projects" (stat blocks, exact wording, use verbatim — do not round or embellish). No RERA, no awards, no press — be honest about this in the final report.

## The gap the redesign has to close
The current site is a generic WordPress/Elementor template ("Gutenify/Gutener Business" demo) with unreplaced Lorem-ipsum testimonials, blank "Name/Designation" team cards, a placeholder FAQ page, and stock demo photography still live in production. It reads as unfinished, not as "dated but real." The new site's job is to make the *real* material — the renders, the "Creation, Not Construction" line, the Vikramshila name — carry all the weight, with nothing invented to paper over the gaps (no RERA, no team names, no testimonials beyond the hotel's three real ones).

## The ONE hero asset
**`images/optimized/project-image-1.jpg`** (source: Shivalaya "day/dusk view," 1600×809) — a twilight CGI render: deep indigo-to-lavender sky, the building's windows lit warm gold, palm-lined street in the foreground. It's the widest (2:1), most cinematic-aspect frame in the harvest, with the clearest light/dark separation for a particle-resolve treatment, and the only one that reads as "monument at dusk" rather than "brochure render." A small "shivalaya" wordmark is baked into the top-left corner of the source file — the site-wide hero crop excludes it (crop starts right of that corner); the full uncropped file is reused as-is on the Shivalaya project page itself, where the mark belongs.

Runner-up material for the home page's featured-projects montage: Annapurna Heights (day/night/aerial trio — best *set* of consistent angles for one project) and Chandeshwar Apartment's night view (strongest single alternate hero).
