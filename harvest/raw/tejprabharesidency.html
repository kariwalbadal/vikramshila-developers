Tejprabha Residency

Welcome to Tejprabha Residency.
It’s a way of life.

An embodiment of world-class living and comfort, Tejprabha Residency is a housing project being developed by Vikramshila Developers in Purnia, Bihar.
Committed to providing the excellence of urban architecture with an emphasis on luxurious living, the flats are designed to provide unmatched comfort.
Tejprabha Residency is a high-end residential development housing spacious
2BHK and 3BHK flats, built to exemplary construction standards. Keeping the
safety of its residents in minds, Vikramshila Developers, who are renowned for their quality construction and attention to detail, has ensured that the structure
is earthquake-resistant. It is a fully integrated, extremely well-planned apartment
with best-in-class amenities. An ultra-modern gated community, it is tastefully
designed to offer sophisticated homeowners a contemporary take on luxury and
convenience.
Private and delightfully crafted, this majestic residency is extraordinary in every sense of the world.

Project Highlights

  • Earthquake Resistance Structure
  • Generator Backup
  • Security Cabin intercom facility
  • Elegant Designer Entrance Gate
  • Ample Car Parking
  • 24×7 Water Supply
  • State Of Art Elevators
  • Rain Water Harvesting
  • Internal Wall Finish With Smooth Gypsum Platter
