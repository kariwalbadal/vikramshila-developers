Ganesh Enclave

Own a house in a locality that is away from the maddening traffic and frenzied activity and close to everything that is green and clean, Ganesh Enclaves is the right answer.

These prestigious 72 flats having a breath-taking ambiance is indeed every nature lover’s delight providing amenities like a swimming pool to keep you cool, landscaped gardens to let your hair down and play with your children, a fully equipped gymnasium, community hall, children’s play area and many more, So come and explore Ganesh Enclaves and discover that there are many more reasons to make it your own.

It’s a location that’s perfect in more ways than one located at Sitla Asthan chowk , Mirzan Hat Road, Bhagalpur provides easy access to market place,railway station and highway. Upcoming flyover over Bholanath Bridge will no doubt provide investment benefits in future.
