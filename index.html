The Standard

What every home gets.

Six things repeated, unprompted, in the written specifications of every ground we’ve built — not marketing copy. The plan itself.

01

Earthquake-resistant framing

Not an upgrade — the frame every one of our recent structures is engineered to first, in a state that feels it.

02

Round-the-clock security

CCTV surveillance and trained security are written into the plan before the first flat is sold, not added after residents ask.

03

Generator backup, every home

Common areas, lifts and individual flats — power stays on, on every ground we build.

04

Granite, not laminate

Kitchen platforms in granite with steel sinks — the specification sheet, not the brochure copy, is where this is decided.

05

Vitrified tile flooring

Throughout, not just in the rooms a buyer walks during a site visit.

06

Rain water harvesting

Where the site allows it, it goes in — a small line item that outlasts most of what gets marketed harder.
